This project focuses on the visualization and analytical reporting of the cleaned financial dataset developed during Week 2 for TCS, Infosys, HCLTech, and Wipro. The primary objective was to convert structured quarterly financial data into clear, decision-oriented visualizations that could help identify trends, compare peer performance, and communicate financial insights effectively.

Using the standardized dataset covering 13 common fiscal quarters from Q1 FY24 to Q1 FY27, I developed a range of financial charts, tables, and comparative visualizations covering revenue, revenue growth, operating profitability, net profit, margins, and peer-level performance. The visualizations were designed from an equity-research perspective, with emphasis on comparability, trend identification, and interpretation rather than simply presenting numerical information.

Time-series visualizations were used to examine quarterly movements and identify periods of acceleration, moderation, or volatility in financial performance. A revenue-index view was added to compare relative growth from a common Q1 FY24 base, while growth-volatility analysis was used to assess the stability of quarter-to-quarter revenue changes. Margin charts were retained with an explicit basis note because the standardized Week 2 operating-profit field is not identical to every issuer's own EBIT or IT-services margin definition.

Each visualization was accompanied by an analytical interpretation explaining the trend, the underlying pattern, and its relevance to financial performance assessment. The Q1 FY27 issuer-validated snapshot was presented separately from the historical standardized panel so that current company-defined reporting bases were not mixed silently with the historical extraction layer.

A final review and refinement stage focused on consistent units, readable axis labels, non-misleading scales, limited visual clutter, and clear source/basis annotations. The completed report provides a reproducible visualization workflow and creates the analytical handoff for Week 4 forecasting and predictive modelling.
